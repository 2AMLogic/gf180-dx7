# DR-0015: H03 frame-budget reconciliation with SPI event admission, queue carry-over, and §6 STALE disposition

- **DR number:** 0015
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Deciders:** loom-builder (agent); review and ratification per `docs/CONTRACT-CORE-v1.md` §6 and #336
- **Related issues:** #339 (this amendment), #25 (H03), #264 / PR #336 (DR-0008 clock-target ratification), #29 (H07), #30 (H08), #37 (U04)
- **Amends:** `docs/CONTRACT-CORE-v1.md`, `spec/core-contract-v1.json`, `spec/core-contract-v1.signoff.json`, `tools/h03_budget_check.py` (adopted under DR-0008)

## Context

1. **Why a post-implementation amendment is required.** During the EE ratification review of DR-0008 (PR #336), two omissions in the original H03 schedule budget (`docs/CONTRACT-CORE-v1.md` §2, `spec/core-contract-v1.json` `budget_worst_case`, `tools/h03_budget_check.py`) were identified:
   - **Arbitrary-phase SPI completion bound:** `write_queue_drain` used whole-period floor division `floor(W / 194)` (`84` at `W = 16,384`, `168` at `W = 32,768`), omitting a transaction already in flight across the opening frame boundary (`offset 0`). In a half-open frame `[0, W)` (where a completion at `offset W` belongs to the next frame `[W, 2W)`), completions spaced by `>= 194` core clocks can fall at offsets `0, 194, ..., 194 * floor((W - 1) / 194)`, giving **`1 + floor((W - 1) / 194)`** = **`85`** completions at 12.288 MHz (`16,296 < 16,384 <= 16,490`) and **`169`** completions at 24.576 MHz (`32,592 < 32,768 <= 32,786`).
   - **Event admission vs polyphony bound:** H03 priced `16` note-ons + `16` note-offs + `16` controller events (`4,864` clocks) as the worst admissible burst by equating note-on commands with 16-voice polyphony (DEC-003). On the wire interface of `rtl/dx7_core.v` (`sha256: f33cecbd138aea2a869cb502dca9273b342c8f97be51c9a1e587f2eb4aba89fa`), each raw `SEC=0, A=0x00` (`EA_NOTEON`) transaction is a single 48-bit write entering a 168-entry event queue (`EVQ_DEPTH = 168`) and dispatching to `F_ALLOC` even when all 16 voices are occupied. Even 100 raw note-ons with the original non-event rows demand `17,344 - 4,864 + 100 * 224 = 34,880` planning clocks (`-2,112` margin at 24.576 MHz).

2. **As-built skid, event-queue, and allocator mechanics (`rtl/dx7_core.v`).**
   - **Skid stage (`skid[0:7]`, depth 8 × 49 b):** `wr_wr` (`skid_push`) asserts one cycle after the 48th synchronized `sck_rise`; `skid_pop = (skid_cnt != 0)` drains unconditionally at 1 write/clock into the `SEC=1` patch shadow or the `SEC=0` event queue (`evq`). At compliant spacing (`>= 194` core clocks/write), `skid_cnt <= 1` on every reachable cycle (`skid_full` is unreachable; `skid_rej = 0`), carrying at most **1** write in flight across a frame boundary (`skid_push` at `offset = W - 1` drains via `skid_pop` at `offset = 0` of the next frame). Because `skid_pop` is a rigid `+1`-clock shift of `skid_push`, `skid_pop` drains in `[0, W)` are also bounded by `1 + floor((W - 1) / 194)` (`85` / `169`).
   - **Event queue (`evq[0:167]`, `EVQ_DEPTH = 168`):** `SEC=0` drains (`skid_pop && !dr_sec`) write `evq` when `!evq_full` (`evq_cnt < 168`). A write presented when `evq_cnt == 168` (`evq_full == 1`) sets sticky `st_overflow <= 1` and is **rejected at the `evq` stage even if `wc_pop_fire == 1` on the same clock** (`rtl/dx7_core.v:1445,1566,1580`), while that cycle's pop reduces `evq_cnt` to `167` and frees space for *subsequent* cycles.
   - **Drain-until-empty event application (`F_EVPOP`) and strike timing:** At `frame_tick`, `fc_state` enters `F_COMMIT` (`offset 0`) and then `F_EVPOP` (`offset 1`), looping until `evq_empty` (`evq_cnt == 0`) rather than snapshotting `evq_cnt` at `frame_tick`. On any non-overrunning prior frame, `env_busy == 0` at frame start (`F_ENVW`), and every occupied sounding strike (`kind == 1` via `match_found` or `steal_found`) takes **26 core clocks** (`F_EVPOP` 1 + `F_ALLOC` 1 + `F_NN_BP` 1 + `F_NN_BPW` 6 + `F_NN_ENVP` 6 + `F_NN_ENVK` 6 + `F_NN_ENVK4` 3 + `F_NN_PEG` 1 + `F_NN_BOOK` 1; under the silent reset patch where `sounding == 0 && in_rel == 0`, `F_ALLOC` exits in 2 clocks with 0 strikes). Consequently, pops during `F_EVPOP` free queue slots for mid-frame `SEC=0` arrivals before `evq` hits empty:
     - **24.576 MHz (`W = 32,768`):** `Q_open = 168` (filled in Frame `N - 1`) blocks admissions at offsets `0` and `1`; `169` Frame-`N` completions draining at offsets `2, 196, ..., 32,594` are all admitted (`0` rejections), `26` arrive before `F_EVPOP` empties at offset `5,045` (`2 + 25 * 194 = 4,852 <= 26 * 193 = 5,018 < 5,046 = 2 + 26 * 194`), yielding **`194` completed strikes** (`168 + 26`) and closing occupancy **`Q_close = 143`**. (`Q_open <= 167` allows `d_1 >= 0`, giving at most `167 + 26 = 193`.)
     - **12.288 MHz (`W = 16,384`, paper window on the same FSM from a non-overrunning prior frame):** Frame `N - 1` exits `F_EVPOP` at `>= -16,383`, admitting up to `Q_open = 85` drains (`< 168`, not full); phase continuity gives `d_1 >= 108` (`E_admitted = 84`, `13` mid-pop admissions -> **`98` strikes**, `Q_close = 71`; `Q_open = 84` with `d_1 = 0`, `E_admitted = 85`, `14` mid-pop admissions likewise gives **`98` strikes**, `Q_close = 71`).
     - **Mixed 24.576 MHz regression:** In `carry_cc123_337`, CC123 replaces only the first write. With slot 15 held, the all-notes-off scan wraps and delays exit from `F_EVPOP`. Frame 4 admits 169 writes and pops 195 entries from an opening queue of 168 (194 strikes and one CC123), closing at 142. Frame 5 consumes the remaining 142. This costs `194 × 224 + 32 = 43,488` event clocks. The all-note-on control costs 43,456. Both frames have zero commits. The tight mixed-event maximum is unresolved at both clocks.
   - **Unresolved paper conservation upper bound:** Under H03's `derived_estimate` unit cost of `224` clk/strike (`224 > 194`), service would be slower than SPI arrival so `evq` would not empty mid-frame; pure queue conservation (`Q_open + E_admitted`) bounds queued note-ons in one frame by **`168 + 169 = 337`** at 24.576 MHz (`87,969` clocks, margin `-55,201`) and **`85 + 84 = 169`** from a clean prior frame at 12.288 MHz (`50,253` clocks, margin `-33,869`; or `168 + 85 = 253` from an already-full `evq`, `69,069` clocks, margin `-52,685`). Because every reachable raw-wire witness from 100 note-ons upward already exceeds `32,768` clocks, the planning verdict at both clocks is **FAIL** regardless of whether the 194-strike RTL-timed witness or the 337-event conservation bound is used.

3. **Raw wire commands vs supplied host (`src/gf180_dx7/host.py`).**
   `EmbeddedHost` (`sha256: 4e7457a852227afd66f0502bfbe85dd92955144e232274a0063958261d3022fc`) reserves `STATUS_POLL_WINDOWS = 1` out of `floor(W / 194)` (`write_cap = 83` at 12.288 MHz, `167` at 24.576 MHz) and emits **8** host writes per note-on (`1` velocity + `6` per-op detune deltas + `1` command = `224 / 8 = 28` planning clk/write) and **1** write per note-off (`48` clk/write) or controller (`32` clk/write), so the historical 16/16/16 vector takes **160** host writes (`161` windows incl. poll; refused at 12.288 MHz, admitted at 24.576 MHz).
   - **Wire encoding.** U04 serializes note-off 60 as `00000002003c` and its status poll as `800000000000`. The RTL decodes writes with bit 47 set and selects the event by address. A sequence of those host note-offs followed by that poll presents just one RTL write, to the note-on address. Direct supplied-host timing has **NO_VERDICT** at both clocks.
   - **Conditional note-off pricing.** Assume translation into RTL note-offs, preservation of the host window counts, and the 17-clock unmatched `F_OFFFIND` scan. Single-frame 83 / 167 note-offs demand 16,381 / 20,497 clocks. With carry-over, 91 / 183 pops demand **16,765 (−381, FAIL)** at 12.288 MHz and **21,265 (+11,503, PASS)** at 24.576 MHz. These margins apply to the translated scenarios.


## Decision

1. **Requirements and clock target stand unchanged.** The 16-voice, six-operator, 32-algorithm, 48 kHz product requirements (DEC-002, DEC-003, DEC-009) and the **24.576 MHz design point** (with 12.288 MHz worst-case-infeasible; DR-0008 / #336) are unchanged. No RTL admission, queue depth, latency, host protocol, or overflow policy is altered here; any policy change remains an operator escalation item.
2. **Normative H03 budget reconciliation (`docs/CONTRACT-CORE-v1.md`, `spec/core-contract-v1.json`, `spec/core-contract-v1.signoff.json`).**
   - `write_queue_drain` is amended to **`1 + floor((W - 1) / 194)`** = **`85`** clocks at 12.288 MHz and **`169`** clocks at 24.576 MHz.
   - `budget_worst_case.rows` prices witnesses with one reserved patch commit and the arbitrary-phase drain allowance. At **12.288 MHz**, the analytical 98-strike mix needs **34,349 / 16,384** clocks (margin **−17,965**, **FAIL**). At **24.576 MHz**, the 194-strike plus CC123 mix needs **55,969 / 32,768** clocks (margin **−23,201**, **FAIL**). Pricing the actual mixed frame with 169 drains and zero commits gives **55,905** (margin **−23,137**). Event work alone exceeds each window, even if the patch-commit reserve is omitted.
   - The **historical 16/16/16 vector** is retained as an explicit regression under both whole-period drain (`17,260` / `-876` FAIL; `17,344` / `+15,424` PASS) and arbitrary-phase drain (`17,261` / `-877` FAIL; `17,345` / `+15,423` PASS), alongside the **EE 100-note-on witness** (`34,880` whole-period / `-2,112`; `34,648` isolated app frame / `-1,880`), the **168-note-on opening-queue witness** (`50,113` / `-17,345`; `49,880` isolated app frame / `-17,112`), the **337-event paper conservation upper bound** (`87,969` / `-55,201`), and **translated host note-off pricing** (`FAIL` at 12.288 MHz, margin `-381`; `PASS` at 24.576 MHz, margin `+11,503` for this translated scenario only).
3. **Executable checker gates (`tools/h03_budget_check.py`).** The checker independently recomputes all rows, regression vectors, raw-wire witnesses, supplied-host bounds, and probe conservation laws, compares the published disposition with the computed planning verdicts, observed frames and coverage, accepts a correctly recorded negative planning margin, and **rejects** both a restored whole-period drain bound (`84 / 168`) and any restored all-mixes `PASS` at 24.576 MHz (citing the 100-note-on EE witness `34,880` clocks, `-2,112` margin, and the 98/194 reachable strike counts).
4. **§6 STALE disposition and separate RTL deadline/overflow reporting.**
   - **Marked `STALE` (for all-admissible H03 schedule/burst completeness claims only):** H07's schedule-completeness and 167-write `stress-burst` claim (`docs/H07-CORE.md` §2, `evidence/h07-core/results-dr0012-p{1,2}.json`, `evidence/h07-core/stress/manifest.json`) and H08's 168-period SPI burst-bound assumptions (`docs/H08-PIN.md`, `tests/test_h08.py`). RTL-vs-frozen-model PCM bit-exactness on the exercised vectors and H01/H02/H04/H05/H06 slice results are unaffected (`rtl/dx7_core.v` bytes unchanged).
   - **Measured RTL deadline and overflow (`evidence/h03-event-admission/results.json`):**
     - *24.576 MHz exercised SPI probes (9 runs, Verilator 5.050):* **PASS** on exercised cases — `OVERRUN = 0` in every run (`max busy_clks = 8,290 / 32,768`, slack `24,478` clocks on the mixed CC123 frame); `OVERFLOW = 0` on 100, 168, `skid_straddle_169`, `carry_replenish_337`, and `carry_cc123_337`; `OVERFLOW = 1` on `burst_169_sustained`, `phase_max_170`, and `sim_pop_reject_170`, with every rejection occurring at `event_queue` (`evq_full`) and `0` at `skid_fifo`.
     - *24.576 MHz all-admissible-input RTL deadline:* **`NO_VERDICT`** (exhaustive WCET across all 32 algorithms, 96-op AM-sensitivity `exp()` paths, `refresh_pending`, and controller/note-off mixes is `NOT_RUN`).
     - *12.288 MHz RTL deadline and overflow:* **`NOT_RUN`** (`rtl/dx7_core.v` hardcodes 512 clocks/sample = 32,768 clocks/frame; all-input verdict **`NO_VERDICT`**).

5. **Normative interface conformance.** DEC-014 / NUM-011 requires events received in frame N to first affect sample 0 of N+1. Frame 4 of `carry_replenish_337` instead applies 26 same-frame admissions. DEC-014 requires clear-on-read overflow status and host retry. H08 F-OVF-1 observes sticky overflow through successive reads until pad reset. Both rules have observed conformance gaps. Policy changes require an operator decision.

## Consequences

- **Enables:** honest, machine-checked separation between (a) all-admissible wire planning (`FAIL` at both clocks under H03 estimates), (b) historical 16/16/16 and conditional translated note-off planning (`PASS` at 24.576 MHz), and (c) measured RTL deadline behavior (`OVERRUN = 0` on exercised 24.576 MHz probes; `NO_VERDICT` across all inputs), unblocking clean downstream binding in #336 and #264.
- **Forbids:** claiming `PASS` for all wire-admissible frames at 24.576 MHz under the H03 unit-cost estimates, using `floor(W / 194)` as an arbitrary-phase SPI completion bound, or treating exercised `OVERRUN = 0` simulation runs as an all-input WCET proof.
- **Closing the all-input guarantee requires one of two future steps:** (1) replacing H03's `derived_estimate` rows with a proven RTL worst-case cycle bound across all reachable states, or (2) an operator-ratified admission/protocol change under the H03 escalation rule.
